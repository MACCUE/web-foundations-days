About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight web application designed to help you organize thoughts quickly. It keeps your daily reminders structured and easily accessible right from your browser.

How to Use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the input box on the Home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button to save it.
  3. Review your active list anytime you revisit the site.

Features

  • Fast and lightweight HTML structure
  • Accessible forms and navigation
  • Clean keyboard-focused workflows
